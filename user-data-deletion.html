User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This page explains how Vitty users can request deletion of personal data associated with a Vitty account, including data received through Google, Apple, Meta, Facebook, or other connected sign-in or integration providers.

How to request deletion

1. Send a deletion request

Email privacy@vitty.ai with the subject line "Vitty User Data Deletion Request".

2. Include account details

Include the email address used for your Vitty account, your name, your firm name if applicable, and the sign-in provider you used, such as email, Google, Apple, Meta, or Facebook.

3. Confirm your authority

If you are requesting deletion for a firm, client, team member, or connected account, include enough information for us to verify that you are authorised to make the request.

4. Watch for confirmation

We will review the request, verify identity or authority where needed, and confirm completion or explain any data that must be retained for legal, accounting, security, dispute, audit-trail, or professional-record reasons.

What may be deleted

Depending on your account and permissions, deletion may include account profile data, sign-in provider identifiers, session records, support messages, conversation history, uploaded source documents, assignment data, imported records, audit-trail data, and other personal data associated with your Vitty account.

What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained or isolated when deletion would conflict with legal obligations, tax or accounting requirements, security investigations, dispute handling, fraud prevention, contractual duties, professional audit trails, or another user's legitimate access to shared firm records.

Connected provider data

If you used Google, Apple, Meta, Facebook, or another provider to sign in or connect data, you may also revoke Vitty's access from that provider's account settings. Revoking access at the provider may stop future sharing, but it may not delete data already stored in Vitty. To delete stored Vitty data, email us using the steps above.

Meta or Facebook app review users

If you reached this page from a Meta or Facebook data deletion flow, email privacy@vitty.ai with the subject line "Meta/Facebook Data Deletion Request" and include the email address or user identifier associated with your Vitty account. We will use the information you provide to locate and delete eligible data.

Expected timing

We aim to process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period and normally within 30 days unless a shorter period is required by law or a longer period is necessary because the request is complex, legally restricted, or requires additional verification.

Questions

For questions about deletion, privacy rights, or account data, contact privacy@vitty.ai. For general support, contact support@vitty.ai.

This page is designed to be used as a public data deletion instructions URL. Review the contact email, timeline, and legal-retention language before publishing.
