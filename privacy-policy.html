Vitty.ai

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Vitty.ai helps CA firms prepare, validate, review, and trace compliance work. This policy explains what personal and business data we collect, why we use it, how we share it, and how users can ask to access, correct, or delete their data.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Vitty websites, applications, product workflows, sign-in flows, support channels, integrations, and related services. It covers data about account users, invited clients, firm team members, and people whose information appears in source documents processed through Vitty.

2. Data we collect

Category Examples
Account data Name, email address, password credentials or sign-in provider, profile picture, user ID, account creation date, firm association, and session information.
Authentication provider data Google or Apple account identifiers, email address, display name, identity tokens, and profile information you authorise the provider to share.
Firm and client workflow data Client names, assignments, filing workflows, GST, TDS, ROC, income tax, audit or workpaper information, source documents, invoices, statements, notes, vouchers, amounts, party names, deadlines, statuses, and review actions.
Conversation and support data Questions, chat messages, selected rows or cells, support requests, feedback, issue reports, and related context needed to respond or improve the service.
Usage and device data Log data, pages or features used, timestamps, browser and device information, IP address, errors, diagnostics, security events, and performance data.

3. How we collect data

We collect data when you create an account, sign in, upload or connect source material, use assignments, tables, audit trails, chat, imports, exports, or other product features, invite users, contact support, or interact with our website. We may also receive data from authorised third-party services you connect to Vitty.

4. Why we use data

We use data to provide and secure Vitty, authenticate users, operate workflows, prepare and validate compliance work, reconcile records, surface exceptions and assumptions, maintain evidence trails, respond to support requests, improve product reliability, prevent misuse, comply with legal obligations, and communicate service updates.

5. AI processing

Vitty may use AI systems to assist with preparation, reconciliation, explanation, drafting, validation, query handling, and exception review. AI outputs are assistive and must be reviewed by authorised humans before professional reliance, filing, signature, or client delivery.

6. How we share data

We do not sell user or client data. We may share data with service providers that help us host, authenticate, secure, analyse, support, or operate Vitty; with integrations you authorise; with your firm administrators or authorised collaborators; with professional advisers; or with regulators, courts, or law enforcement where required by law.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect account credentials, limit access to authorised personnel, and promptly remove access for people who no longer need it.

8. Data retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide Vitty, maintain assignments and audit trails, support users, comply with tax, accounting, legal, security, or professional-record obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete, anonymise, or isolate it according to our retention procedures and applicable law.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and relationship to the data, you may request access, correction, deletion, export, restriction, withdrawal of consent, or information about how personal data is used. To make a request, contact privacy@vitty.ai. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting on a request.

10. User data deletion

You can request deletion of your Vitty account data or data received through a connected sign-in provider. Instructions are available on the User Data Deletion Instructions page. Some data may be retained where required for legal, accounting, security, dispute, audit-trail, or professional-record reasons.

11. Children

Vitty is intended for professional and business use. It is not directed to children, and users should not submit personal data of children unless they have a lawful basis and explicit professional need to do so.

12. International processing

Vitty may process data using infrastructure, service providers, or support personnel located in India or other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border processing and transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Vitty changes or as legal, security, or operational requirements evolve. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify users through the service, email, or website.

14. Contact

For privacy requests, deletion requests, or questions about this policy, contact privacy@vitty.ai. For general support, contact support@vitty.ai.
